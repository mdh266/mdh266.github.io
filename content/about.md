## About Me
------------
I work as an AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist in New York City. Over the past decade, I've worked at the intersection of AI, data science, and software engineering by developing machine learning systems to solve complex, real-world problems. My experience spans a range of disciplines, including optimization, econometrics, entity resolution, geospatial analytics, marketing, and finance. I've also had the opportunity to lead technical teams, mentor and train more than 80 data scientists, and develop machine learning platforms used by over 100 model developers.

I hold a PhD in <a href="https://repositories.lib.utexas.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/ee56a75d-c4e1-4b65-83b7-5ab8e7de9ddc/content">PhD in Computational Applied Mathematics</a> from the <a href="https://oden.utexas.edu/">University of Texas at Austin</a> where my research focused on numerical methods in semiconductor physics with applications to Solar Energy. That background gave me a deep appreciation for mathematical modeling, scientific computing, and the process of turning abstract ideas into practical solutions. It's a perspective I continue to bring to my work today.

For 17 years, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu was a major part of my life. As a competitor and coach, I earned a first-degree black belt, won a World Championship in 2012, and medaled twice at the Pan American Championships. I had the opportunity to train with and compete against some of the best athletes in the world. These experiences taught me lasting lessons in discipline, resilience, humility, and continuous improvement that continue to influence how I approach challenges, learning, and teamwork.

Finally, I've served in the <a href="https://cgaux.org/about.php">U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary</a> since 2021. It's been an honor to volunteer in uniform alongside active-duty Coast Guard personnel in support of the service's missions. I take great pride in serving my country, contributing to something larger than myself, and using my time and abilities to make a positive difference in the communities we serve.
