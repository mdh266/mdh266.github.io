## About Me
------------

I’m an AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist based in New York City with over a decade of experience building and scaling machine learning systems that solve real-world problems. My work focuses on end-to-end development; from designing data pipelines and training models to deploying production systems spanning optimization, econometrics, entity resolution, geospatial analysis, marketing and AutoML .

I’ve spent much of my career working with distributed systems (Spark in Python and Scala), building high-performance data and ML pipelines that power applications at scale. More recently, my focus has shifted toward generative AI and LLM-powered systems, where I’ve been developing tools and applications that drive efficiency. Along the way, I’ve led teams, trained 80+ data scientists, built platforms used by 100+ data scientists, and contributed to systems that drive business value.

I hold a PhD in <a href="https://repositories.lib.utexas.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/ee56a75d-c4e1-4b65-83b7-5ab8e7de9ddc/content">PhD in Computational Applied Mathematics</a> from the <a href="https://oden.utexas.edu/">University of Texas at Austin</a> where my research focused on numerical methods in semiconductor physics with applications to Solar Energy. That background continues to shape how I approach problems today by combining strong mathematical intuition with practical engineering.

Outside of work, I have served in the <a href="https://cgaux.org/about.php">U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary</a> since 2021. For 17 years, I was a competitive Brazilian Jiu Jitsu athlete and coach, winning the world championships in 2012 and a first-degree black belt in 2015. These experiences still influence how I think about growth, learning, leadership and teamwork.
